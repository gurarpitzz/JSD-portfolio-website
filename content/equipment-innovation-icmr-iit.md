# Adaptive Innovation: Indigenous BC3 Boccia Ramp
**Collaborative Technological Breakthrough by BSFI, ICMR & IIT Madras**

---

## The Challenge: Prohibitive Costs of Adaptive Gear
In Paralympic Boccia, athletes competing in the **BC3 category** have severe physical limitations affecting all four extremities. Because they cannot physically grasp or propel a Boccia ball, they use an **assistive ramp** and a **head- or mouth-pointer**.

Historically, high-precision international ramps manufactured in Europe or South Korea carry price tags ranging between €2,500 and €5,000 (₹2.5 to ₹5 Lakh INR), excluding steep import duties and shipping. For most aspiring Indian para-athletes—many coming from rural and working-class backgrounds—this economic hurdle was an absolute dead end.

---

## The Breakthrough Collaboration
Determined to dismantle this financial barrier, BSFI President **Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal** initiated a joint engineering endeavor uniting:
* **Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI):** Domain rules, athlete ergonomics, and field testing.
* **Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR):** Biomechanical assessment and institutional research backing.
* **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras:** Advanced materials engineering, precision CNC manufacturing, and kinematics modeling.

---

## Engineering Specifications & Design Features
The indigenous BC3 Boccia Ramp was designed to meet strict **World Boccia Rulebook (Section 11)** standards:
1. **Ultra-Low Friction Chute:** High-density polymer sliding surface treated for consistent roll velocity and trajectory retention.
2. **Modular Height & Angle Adjustment:** Micro-adjustable elevation mechanisms allowing smooth single-handed operation by sports assistants.
3. **Lightweight Aeronautical Alloy Frame:** High strength-to-weight ratio ensuring structural rigidity without exceeding transport weight thresholds.
4. **Universal Pointer Interface:** Ergonomically custom-molded head pointers and mouth attachments tailored to individual athlete cranial profiles.

---

## Unveiling & Field Validation
* **Demonstration at ICMR Headquarters (September 2026):**  
  Formally demonstrated before senior medical directors, bioengineers, and BSFI leadership in New Delhi.
* **Validation at IAMD Solan Coaching Camp:**  
  Rigorous multi-week field testing under tournament conditions with international athletes including Sachin Chamaria and Ajeya Raj.
* **Global Compliance:** Certified fully compliant with World Boccia dimensional and gradient tolerances, ready for international competitive deployment.
