# Athletes & Podium Legacy: Champions Mentored by BSFI
**Celebrating India's Elite International Boccia Medalists & World-Stage Triumphs**

---

## Overview
Under the leadership and continuous mentorship of **Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal**, the Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI) has identified, trained, and elevated athletes with severe motor disabilities (Cerebral Palsy, Muscular Dystrophy, Quadriplegia, Hereditary Neuropathy) from grassroots communities into international champions winning on the global World Boccia circuit.

## Hall of Fame Athletes & Recent World Triumphs

### 1. Ajeya Raj (BC3) — "The Golden Boy"
* **Classification:** BC3 Male Individual & Pairs (Ramp Division)
* **Historic Milestones:**
  * **5 Gold Medal Sweep at Pilsen 2026 World Boccia Challenger (Czech Republic):** Historic multi-medal performance alongside Anjali Thakur, bringing India to top global standing.
  * **Dominant Egyptian Campaign (Cairo 2026):** 10–0 shutout victory over UAE in opening match, followed by victory over Egypt in round two.
  * **Bronze Medal:** World Boccia Challenger (Manama, Bahrain 2024). Known for tactical jack placement and cool-headed ramp precision.

### 3. Pooja Gupta (BC4) — Pioneer International Medalist
* **Hometown:** Rewari, Haryana
* **Condition:** Hereditary Motor Sensory Neuropathy
* **Historic Milestones:**
  * **India's First-Ever Individual International Boccia Medalist:** Captured the **Bronze Medal** in BC4 Female Individual at the Bahrain 2024 World Boccia Challenger.
  * **Silver Medalist:** Teamed with Jatin Kumar Kushwaha in BC4 Pairs in Bahrain.
  * **Multi-time National Champion:** Gold medalist in Gwalior, Chandigarh, and New Delhi.
* **Quote by Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal:**  
  > *"Pooja Gupta's historic triumph in Bahrain shattered the ceiling for Indian Boccia. She proved to the entire nation that physical limitations can never restrain relentless human will."*
* **Featured Image:** [Pooja Gupta BC4](file:///D:/Portfolio-jaspreet%20boccia/assets/images/star-players/pooja-gupta-bc4.webp)
