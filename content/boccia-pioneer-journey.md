# The Boccia Movement in India: Pioneering a Paralympic Discipline
**History, Governance, and Global Footprint of the Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI)**

---

## What is Boccia?
Boccia (pronounced *bot-cha*) is a Paralympic precision ball sport contested by athletes with severe physical disabilities affecting motor function across all four limbs. Originating in ancient Greece and formalized in Europe in the 1970s, it made its Paralympic debut at the **1984 New York Paralympic Games**. 

Today, governed globally by **World Boccia** and contested in over 75 countries, it stands as the gold standard of true athletic inclusion.

### The Sport's Precision Mechanics
* **Court:** Flat, smooth indoor surface measuring $12.5\text{ m} \times 6\text{ m}$.
* **The Play:** Players throw, kick, or use an assistive ramp and pointer to propel leather balls as close as possible to a white target ball, known as the **"jack"**.
* **Categories:**
  * **BC1:** Severe quadriplegia / cerebral palsy with manual throwing ability; assisted by an on-court helper.
  * **BC2:** Severe motor impairment; throw balls without an assistant.
  * **BC3:** Severe locomotor dysfunction in all four limbs (e.g. high-level spinal cord injury, SMA, severe CP). Compete using an **assistive ramp** and a head/mouth pointer, working in tandem with a Sports Assistant who sits with their back to the court.
  * **BC4:** Severe physical disabilities not of cerebral origin (e.g. muscular dystrophy, limb deficiency).

## The Genesis in India (2016)
Before 2016, India had no formal Boccia presence. Athletes living with quadriplegia and severe muscular dystrophy had virtually no avenue to participate in national or international sports.

Following his vision sparked at the 2014 Seoul Taekwondo tournament, **Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal** founded the **Para Boccia Sports Welfare Society** in 2016. He personally funded initial equipment sets, conducted sensitization workshops, and drafted the first standard operating rules in Hindi and English.

What started with a handful of enthusiastic athletes has expanded into the **Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI)**, recognized by:
* **World Boccia** (International Governing Body)
* **Paralympic Committee of India (PCI)**
* **Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports (MYAS), Government of India**
* **Sports Authority of India (SAI)**

---

## Expanding Across the Republic
Under Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal's presidency, BSFI built:
1. **Interactive State Association Network:** Officially affiliated state chapters across Maharashtra, Haryana, Delhi, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, and beyond.
2. **National Championships:** 10+ editions of Sub-Junior, Junior, and Senior National Championships, with the 11th Edition scheduled for 2027.
3. **National Coaching Camps:** High-performance residential camps (such as at IAMD, Solan, Himachal Pradesh) supported by SAI and MYAS, incorporating sports science, nutrition, wheelchair ergonomics, and tactical analysis.
4. **Awareness Outreach:** Direct partnership with Spinal Cord Injury (SCI) networks, organizing demonstrations and mass inclusion days.

---

## International Milestones & Federation Governance
* **World Boccia Annual General Meeting 2026 (Seoul, South Korea):**  
  BSFI President Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal and Chairman Ashok Bedi represented India at the highest legislative assembly of World Boccia, advocating for quota allocations, regional hosting rights in India, and bilateral coach exchanges.
* **The Historic Bahrain 2024 Campaign:**  
  Under Dhaliwal's on-site leadership, India's 7-member squad delivered a historic masterclass at the World Boccia Challenger in Manama, capturing **6 international medals**—solidifying India's position on the global map.
* **Continuous Global Circuit:** Fielding teams at the 2026 Cairo World Boccia Challenger and 2026 Pilsen World Boccia Challenger in the Czech Republic.
