## Portfolio of Major Games & Championships

### 1. New Delhi 2026 World Para Athletics Grand Prix
* **Dates:** 8th to 13th March 2026
* **Venue:** Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium, New Delhi
* **Designation:** Member of Local Organising Committee (LOC)
* **Operational Scope:** Core planning, protocol liaison with international delegations, anti-doping coordination, and athlete flow management.

---

### 2. New Delhi 2025 World Para Athletics Championships
* **Dates:** 27th September to 5th October 2025
* **Venue:** Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium, New Delhi
* **Designation:** **Chairman of Transportation Committee, Local Organising Committee (LOC)**
* **Operational Scope & Key Achievements:**
  * Commanded the primary transport operations for one of the largest world championships ever hosted on Indian soil.
  * Mobilized a specialized fleet of accessible buses fitted with hydraulic wheelchair tail-lifts and international safety tie-down mechanisms.
  * Designed end-to-end transport loops between Indira Gandhi International Airport (Terminal 3), designated accessible official hotels across NCR, and the Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium.
  * Maintained zero-incident, on-time departures for thousands of athletes spanning over 100 competing nations.

---

### 3. New Delhi 2025 World Para Athletics Grand Prix
* **Dates:** 8th to 13th March 2025
* **Venue:** Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium, New Delhi
* **Designation:** Member of Local Organising Committee (LOC)
* **Operational Scope:** Multi-functional event delivery, stadium accessibility audits, and athlete village transportation.

### 4. New Delhi 2024 WSPS Para Shooting World Cup
* **Dates:** 6th to 15th March 2024
* **Venue:** Dr. Karni Singh Shooting Range, New Delhi
* **Designation:** Member of Local Organising Committee (LOC)
* **Operational Scope:** Handling high-security firearms transit, specialized ammunition transport clearance, barrier-free access at the ranges, and athlete transit for world-class Paralympic marksmen.

---

### 5. Bahrain 2021 Asian Youth Para Games
* **Dates:** 2nd to 6th December 2021
* **Venue:** Manama, Kingdom of Bahrain
* **Designation:** **Deputy Chef de Mission, Indian Contingent**
* **Operational Scope:**
  * Second-highest executive leader of the entire Indian delegation in Bahrain.
  * Direct liaison with the Asian Paralympic Committee (APC) for entries, technical meetings, classification reviews, and emergency response.
  * Spearheaded daily team manager briefings and athlete support services, culminating in a record medal haul for India's youth contingent.
