## Executive Profile Summary
Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal is the visionary sports administrator who introduced, structured, and championed the Paralympic sport of Boccia across India. In 2016, following international exposure to disability athletics, he established the Para Boccia Sports Welfare Society (now the Boccia Sports Federation of India - BSFI), building a nationwide ecosystem comprising medical athlete classification, grassroots talent development, coaching certifications, and world-class competition infrastructure. 

Beyond Boccia, he has served as a core leader in the Paralympic Committee of India (PCI), holding high-stakes appointments including Deputy Chef de Mission of the Indian Team at the Bahrain 2021 Asian Youth Para Games, Chairman of the Transportation Committee for the New Delhi 2025 World Para Athletics Championships, and Key LOC Member for multiple World Para Athletics Grand Prix and National Championships.

---

## The Spark of Inception: 2014 Seoul Taekwondo
In 2014, while attending an international Taekwondo championship in Seoul, South Korea, Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal encountered a sport that would alter the trajectory of Indian disability sports forever: **Boccia**.

He watched athletes with the most severe neuro-muscular conditions—quadriplegia, severe cerebral palsy, muscular dystrophy, and catastrophic spinal trauma—competing with razor-sharp tactical precision, emotional intensity, and national pride. In India at that time, sports were largely unavailable to individuals with high support needs. Those who could not run, jump, or throw a javelin were largely excluded from competitive athletics.

Dhaliwal made a solemn pledge: **to bring Boccia home to India and build an unshakeable institutional framework so that every Indian with severe motor impairment would have a podium to strive for.**

## Laying the National Foundations (2016 – Present)
In 2016, Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal formally introduced Boccia to India, founding the **Para Boccia Sports Welfare Society**, which subsequently matured into the **Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI)**. 

Starting from zero athletes, zero certified ramps, and zero domestic awareness, Dhaliwal embarked on relentless nationwide travel:
1. **Athlete Identification & Medical Classification:** Conducting grassroots talent hunts across hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and special schools.
2. **Coach and Referee Accreditation:** Organizing training seminars aligning Indian officials with World Boccia rules and IPC standards.
3. **State Association Network:** Establishing recognized state Boccia bodies across North, South, West, and Eastern India.
4. **National Championships:** Instituting annual National Sub-Junior, Junior, and Senior Boccia Championships, providing hundreds of para-athletes their first taste of national competition.

## Global Recognition & Championship Triumphs
Under Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal's presidency, India's international Boccia trajectory has been meteoric:
* **2018 Jakarta Asian Para Games:** India's historic debut on the Asian Paralympic stage (Jaspreet served as Head Coach).
* **2019 Fazza Cup (Dubai):** Testing India's tactical prowess on the international circuit.
* **2021 Asian Youth Para Games (Bahrain):** Appointed **Deputy Chef de Mission** of the entire Indian Contingent, steering youth athletes across multiple sports to glory.
* **2024 World Boccia Challenger (Bahrain):** A watershed milestone where Team India swept **6 historic medals** (1 Gold, 1 Silver, 4 Bronze), including India's first-ever individual international Boccia medal won by Pooja Gupta (BC4).
* **2026 World Boccia Championship (Seoul, South Korea):** Led Team India to their **historic first appearance** at the World Boccia Championship in August 2026.
* **2026 Pilsen World Boccia Challenger (Czech Republic):** Swept **5 GOLD MEDALS** in a historic comeback led by Ajeya Raj and Anjali Thakur.
* **2026 Cairo World Boccia Challenger (Egypt):** Dominated pool play with landmark victories over UAE (10-0), Croatia, Portugal, and Slovenia.
* **2026 Astana World Cup (Kazakhstan):** Led India's elite contenders in high-level World Cup action.

## Breaking Technology Barriers: Indigenous BC3 Ramps
Recognizing that imported assistive Boccia ramps cost thousands of euros—putting the BC3 category out of reach for underprivileged athletes—Dhaliwal forged a pioneering tri-partite research collaboration with the **Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)** and **IIT Madras**. 

Together, they engineered India's first world-class, cost-effective indigenous Boccia ramp, formally handed over to Indian Army Boccia players at ICMR Headquarters in September 2026 in the presence of Secretary DHR & DG ICMR Dr. Rajiv Bahl, Additional DG Dr. Sanghmitra Pati, and Senior Scientist Dr. Ravinder Singh.
