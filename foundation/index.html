Established 2016 • Grassroots Genesis

Bringing Paralympic Boccia to Every Indian State

Founded by Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal in 2016 as the Para Boccia Sports Welfare Society and recognized globally as the Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI), our mission is to bring the Paralympic precision sport of Boccia to every individual in India living with quadriplegia, severe cerebral palsy, muscular dystrophy, and catastrophic spinal cord trauma. Starting from zero domestic awareness and zero equipment, BSFI has built an all-India federation across 20+ states, affiliated with World Boccia (BISFed), the Paralympic Committee of India (PCI), MYAS, SAI, and NADA.

Landmark Assistive Innovation • ICMR & IIT Madras

Indigenous BC3 Assistive Ramp & Pointers

In Paralympic Boccia, BC3 athletes with severe locomotor dysfunction in all four limbs compete using an assistive ramp and a head or mouth pointer. Because imported ramps cost €2,500 to €5,000 (₹2.5–₹5 Lakh INR), BSFI President Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal spearheaded a landmark tri-partite engineering collaboration with the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) and IIT Madras. Together, they engineered India’s first World Boccia-compliant, aeronautical-alloy Indigenous BC3 Assistive Ramp—formally handed over to Indian Army Boccia players at ICMR Headquarters in New Delhi in September 2026 alongside DG ICMR Dr. Rajiv Bahl.

Strategic Roadmap

Vision 2026–28

Under President Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal, the Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI) is advancing six strategic pillars to institutionalize Paralympic excellence nationwide:

01
Nationwide Ramp Deployment

Scale nationwide deployment of the ICMR–IIT Madras Indigenous BC3 Assistive Ramp & custom ergonomic head/mouth pointers across all states.

02
State Associations & Medical Hubs

Expand recognized State Boccia Associations and specialized medical classification hubs across all 28 States and 8 Union Territories.

03
High-Performance SAI Camps

Conduct high-performance SAI residential coaching camps (IAMD Solan & regional centers) for the Asian Para Games Aichi-Nagoya.

04
World Boccia Education & Officials

Accredit Indian referees, classifiers, and coaches through World Boccia (BISFed) international education and certification seminars.

05
Barrier-Free Court Infrastructure

Establish dedicated barrier-free Boccia courts at military spinal injury centers, rehabilitation hospitals, and ALIMCO hubs.

06
Paralympic Podium Glory

Propel Team India's BC1–BC4 champions to podium triumphs at the 2026 Asian Para Games and Los Angeles 2028 Paralympics.
