Official Continental Campaign • World Boccia & APC Sanctioned

Asian Para Games 2026 – Indian National Boccia Campaign (Aichi-Nagoya, Japan)

Dates: October 18–24, 2026  |  Venue: Aichi-Nagoya, Japan  |  Leadership Role: Founder & President, Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI)

Under the executive leadership of Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal, Founder and President of the Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI), India is executing its most comprehensive continental preparation program ahead of the 2026 Asian Para Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.

Strategic Roadmap & High-Performance Mandate

  • Continental Qualification & Seeding: Following landmark international podiums in Manama (Bahrain), Cairo (Egypt), and Pilsen (Czech Republic), alongside Asian Para Games selection trials in Astana (Kazakhstan), BSFI has established a meritocratic national selection pipeline across BC1, BC2, BC3, and BC4 classifications.
  • Indigenous Assistive Technology Deployment: Integration of the indigenous BC3 Boccia Assistive Ramp—developed through the historic Memorandum of Agreement between BSFI, the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), and IIT Madras—into elite national camp preparation.
  • World Boccia Technical Compliance: International refereeing standards, sport-class medical classification verification, and psychological conditioning overseen directly by certified World Boccia technical officials.

Premier Domestic Flagship Championship • BSFI Sanctioned

11th National Boccia Championship (2026–2027 Season)

Schedule: November – December 2026  |  Host Authority: Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI)  |  Presiding Officer: Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal, President, BSFI

Building upon a decade of uninterrupted national championships since 2016–17—including the landmark 10th Boccia National Championship at Chitkara University, Punjab and the 9th National Championship at RIMS Imphal, Manipur—the 11th National Boccia Championship represents the apex domestic competition on the Indian Boccia calendar.

Championship Scope & Technical Standards

  • Pan-India & Armed Forces Representation: Over 100 classified athletes representing more than 22 states, union territories, and the Indian Army Mission Olympics wing compete across Individual, Pair, and Team events in BC1, BC2, BC3, and BC4 categories.
  • World Boccia (BISFed) Officiating Protocol: Multi-court call-room inspection, ball-roll testing devices, and international-standard timing systems supervised by World Boccia certified referees and national technical officials trained by Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal.
  • National Squad Selection: Serves as the primary selection trial for the 2027 World Boccia Challenger and World Cup circuit.

2nd Boccia India Federation Cup 2026 – New Delhi

Venue: New Delhi, India  |  Organizing Body: Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI)  |  President: Jaspreet Singh Dhaliwal

Hosted in the national capital under the auspices of the Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI), the 2nd Boccia India Federation Cup brings together the highest-seeded BC1, BC2, BC3, and BC4 athletes in the country for intensive head-to-head competition.

Key Highlights & Institutional Milestones

  • Armed Forces & Dignitary Felicitation: The championship series in New Delhi was marked by the historic visit and felicitation of General N. S. Raja Subramani (PVSM, AVSM, SM, VSM), Chief of Defence Staff, and the handover of indigenous ICMR–IIT Madras BC3 assistive ramps to Indian Army Boccia players.
  • Elite Podium Finishes: International medalists including Ajeya Raj (Indian Army, BC4) and Jatin Kushwah (BC4 Silver Medalist) delivered standout performances under World Boccia competition rules.
